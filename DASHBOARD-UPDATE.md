# Dashboard reading update

Cybersecurity alerts continue arriving without advancing your selection. Press End to select the newest item in the focused panel. Use arrow keys to browse and Enter to expand an alert. History remains limited to 200 items per category; the oldest items expire when that limit is reached.

Quit the dashboard with Q. Extract this update into your existing project folder, replacing dashboard.py, then restart with your usual command:

    .venv\Scripts\python dashboard.py ws://10.0.0.139:81/

No firmware reflash or dependency changes are needed.
